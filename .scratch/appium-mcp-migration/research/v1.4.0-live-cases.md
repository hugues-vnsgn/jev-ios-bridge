## Limits that carry into Phase 4

- **iOS simulator only.** All five are observed single-tap or preparation cases. [Phase 4](../phases/phase-4-acceptance.md) keeps those iOS-simulator-only. They give no Android emulator or physical Android evidence, and no physical iPhone is in scope.
- **They open the real admission store.** Each case prepares a device (create, boot, install, launch) and holds an allocation, so it needs the real admission authority. L04 also quarantines a device. Store activation is a separate owner step, so these cases cannot run before it.
- **Written for the old backend.** The per-backend call counts ("1touch,0relaunch,N≥2captures", "0input,1capture", "0 backend calls") and L04's "legacy successor" step count MobileBuildMCP commands and the old admission binding. Under Appium the counts must be restated as Appium MCP and W3C action calls, and the successor step needs a new meaning before the case runs. Treat the numbers as intent (one touch, no relaunch, no replay), not as thresholds.
- **L04 loses a reply synthetically.** The source says the loss is synthetic reply loss after backend success. It is not a transport severance and not native contact or release settlement.

## Open owner question: what does L04 record for attempted and acknowledged?

Two records of the same lost-reply case disagree.

1. **L04 as specified**, `21fce4965`, `.scratch/observed-action-rollout/next-work.md:121`: "UI_ACTION_UNCONFIRMED, persisted attempted null/acknowledged null, quarantine". `remaining-execution-brief.md:53` in the same commit says "persisted attempted null/acknowledged null, exactly one real touch".
2. **The earlier reply-loss live run**, `2ae75e580` (2026-10-08, "fix: use one held touch for observed iOS navigation"), `.scratch/observed-ui/live/report.md:36`: "The runner recorded `actionAttempted=true`, `actionAcknowledged=false`, and `CONTROLLED_REPLY_DROP_AFTER_REAL_MOBILEBUILDMCP_RESPONSE`; it did not replay." Its table records the terminal record as `inconclusive`, uncertain/quarantined.

Two readings fit, and the sources do not settle which one is meant. The second record may describe the runner's result and the first the persisted terminal record. Or the contract changed between 2026-10-08 and 2026-10-11. On `main`, the golden reply `tests/golden/observed-replies-v1.json` (the `UI_ACTION_UNCONFIRMED` reply, lines 120 to 145) has `actionAttempted: null` and `actionAcknowledged: null`, and `docs/guide/reference/observed-format.md:62` says unknown persisted flags stay null. Neither file shows the runner's own flags.

**Question for the owner:** for the lost-reply case, should Phase 4 expect persisted `attempted`/`acknowledged` of null/null, or true/false? Until answered, the L04 row in the evidence index cannot define a pass.
