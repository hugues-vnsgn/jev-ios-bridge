## Question

What authoritative transition records allow a newly proven native fence to settle a device lease while preserving an inconclusive immutable attempt, historical quarantine, retained allocation capacity and old exclusions?

## Required investigation or discussion

Use ADR-0008/0011 and the existing schema as constraints. Stress-test late replies, lost COMMIT replies, prior-generation proof, abandoned owners and historical native uncertainty. Propose the logical record/state contract for owner review; do not migrate or clear real records.

## Completion criterion

Record a sourced answer or actual owner decision, unresolved limits, and the changes required in the linked phase specification. Keep planning resolution separate from implementation and native acceptance.
