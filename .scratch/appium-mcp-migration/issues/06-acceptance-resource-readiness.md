## Question

What evidence and owner coordination will establish exclusive device assignment and an offline writer window before physical Android acceptance and authority activation?

## Required investigation or discussion

Record a precise owner checklist for a dedicated Android phone, diagnostic app, test scope, custody window, stopped relevant writers, declared historical roots and preserved backups. Connected hardware is not assignment. This ticket permits planning only; do not use a phone, stop another agent or declare quiescence on the owner behalf.

## Completion criterion

Record the owner's agreement on the required readiness evidence, who supplies it, and when it must be revalidated. Update the linked phase specifications with that agreement and any unresolved limits. Closing this ticket records the agreement only. Actual device assignment, writer quiescence and backup evidence must still satisfy the applicable phase entry gates before effects.
