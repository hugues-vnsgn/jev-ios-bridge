## What a v3 case asks

Each case names **one desired screen state at the active checkpoint**. Its goal and assertions describe evidence that could be read on that screen. A value to type is supplied only when this checkpoint needs it; navigation and completion cases use `values: {}`. A screenshot before the desired state has `goal_reached: false` and one or more complete next actions. A screenshot with the full desired state has `goal_reached: true` and `stop-goal`, even if a separate assertion is false. A settled, explicit no-result screen may support `stop-blocked` when the active goal requires opening an existing item. Keep approved labels outside Jev's state.

The corpus remains 10 tuning cases and 20 held-out cases. It uses the v3 corpus schema with the normalized active-checkpoint `Scenario`; it does not send future checkpoints or values to Jev. Keep v2's complete-action option rule and full-only target canonicalization. The model stays `jev-1.13.0`; compare compact/full observations with and without recent history. Retain thresholds 0.6, 0.7, 0.8, and 0.9, Noul yes/no bounds 0.9/0.1, the 255-option limit, and the held-out bar: at least 18/20 correct top choices, at least 16/20 accepted with zero wrong accepted actions, and zero false-pass judgments. Do not tune on held-out outcomes or lower the bar.
