## Unchanged decision gate

Query each of the **20 fresh cases once** with the selected frozen configuration. Require at least 18/20 acceptable top-1 actions before gating; at least 16/20 accepted with zero incorrect accepted actions; and zero false-pass Noul judgments on all known false assertions, even when the action abstains. Request failures stay in the denominator. Report top-1, acceptance and accuracy, false-pass count and known-false count, completion and assertion calibration, latency, tokens, and per-case reasons. A failed second evaluation stays failed. Another revision would need another fresh held-out set; this plan does not pre-authorize repeated peeks or a lower bar. The owner makes the go/no-go call on the new evidence.
