## Traps
- **Occluded screen exposed:** the whole feed list under the sheet is still in the capture as visible tap targets.
- **No tap action on table rows:** Open System Settings, On My iPhone, Add Account, Import/Export Subscriptions are `text` with only `touch`/`longPress`.
- **Icon-only Done** (checkmark).
- **System-leaving row:** "Open System Settings" would leave the app.
- Largest capture here (about 20 KB of Jev text against the 24 KB budget); one more section on screen would risk STATE_BUDGET.
