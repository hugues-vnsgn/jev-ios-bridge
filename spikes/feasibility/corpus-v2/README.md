# Feasibility revision corpus

`corpus.json` holds the original 10 tuning screens and 20 fresh held-out screens. The tuning raw files were copied byte-for-byte from the first corpus and checked against their recorded SHA-256 hashes. Every fresh case has a paired MobileBuildMCP compact/full snapshot with the same `screenHash`, a screenshot, and three asset hashes. Labels remain drafts until the owner reviews and approves this exact corpus and manifest. No v2 Jev request has been made.

The first Settings plan did not match this iOS 26.4 simulator. Settled searches for Display and Notifications each showed no results. Accessibility > Motion displayed a Reduce Motion switch in the screenshot, but MobileBuildMCP exposed no action for it. The paired preflight captures are in `preflight/`. Before any v2 query, the owner replaced those seven slots with Sentry's mock Weather app. Its settings capture exposed actionable Fahrenheit, Celsius, km/h, mph, inHg, and mb controls with selected states. The Weather temperature cases start with Celsius selected and end with Fahrenheit selected. For wind and pressure, the app starts at mph and mb; distance was set to km as unrelated fixture setup so the Settings screen differs from the temperature case. Both orders of changing wind and pressure are acceptable at the untouched Settings screen.

Contacts contains two synthetic Noah Reed records. The Northstar record initially had `noah.old@example.test`; the Harbor record did not. The company is visible in the app screenshot but absent from MobileBuildMCP's text snapshot, so the scored goal identifies the target by its unique old email. Both identical Noah rows are valid inspection actions. Six synthetic phone rows place the email below the edit form's viewport. An upward finger swipe on the form reveals it, and `type-text --replace-existing` changes it to `noah.new@example.test`. The saved card shows the new address. The old-email card was captured before the extra phone rows were added; these cases are independent screen questions, not a continuous recording of one run. Lena Quill was never created; its settled search explicitly says there are no results.

Reminders contains a synthetic list first named Weekend Errands, then renamed Market Errands. Its five captures show the list, More menu, List Info with the old name, List Info with the new unsaved name, and the saved new title. Only the final saved screen asks the deliberately false claim that the old title remains. The R04 full accessibility tree carries an occluded Weekend Errands label marked visible, although its screenshot and compact capture show only the new edit value; that stale label is not scored as proof of a saved title. No truthful `wait` case was available; that category remains unverified.
