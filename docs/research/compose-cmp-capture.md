# Live Compose capture on the owner's demo app

Captured 2026-09-25 for the "Live Compose capture" ticket. Device: the dedicated simulator `0E42FDE2-5E09-42D3-9876-9EF0037FCBE7` (iPhone 17 Pro Max, iOS 26.4, 440×956 pt). Tools: `mobilebuildmcp@2.7.1`, run from this repo's root so `.mobilebuildmcp/config.yaml` applied, with `MOBILEBUILDMCP_SENTRY_DISABLED=true`; bundled AXe 1.8.0. The lock directory `$TMPDIR/jev-ios-bridge-device-locks/` was empty before and after. The bridge was not run, no Jev calls were made, and no product code or app source changed.

**App under test:** `~/Codes/cmp` (not a git repo). Compose Multiplatform **1.11.1** (resolved `org.jetbrains.compose.ui:ui:1.11.1`), Kotlin 2.4.10, Material3 1.11.0-alpha07, `io.github.hugues-vnsgn:number-input:2.2.0`, `dev.viethung:fullscreen-modal:0.1.0` (from `mavenLocal`). The iOS shell is `ContentView` → `UIViewControllerRepresentable` → `ComposeUIViewController { App() }`, with no native navigation or tab chrome. `TEAM_ID` is empty in `iosApp/Configuration/Config.xcconfig`, so the bundle ID is **`org.example.project.cmp`**. The simulator build signs as "Sign to Run Locally", so it needed no team or file change.

What the app contains that matters here:
- **Main screen:** a horizontally scrolling row of six Material `Button`s with `testTag`s (`tabNumberInput` … `toggleTheme`). By default it opens the `modal-perf` screen, which **runs a perf harness about 2 s after it appears**: three modes × two passes, each opening a Compose `Dialog` (the `FullScreenModal` library) holding a 1M-row `LazyColumn`, scrolling it, jumping to the last row, and closing it.
- **Full-screen modal:** the `fullscreen-modal` screen. Its `FullScreenModal` is a Compose `Dialog` on iOS. It holds an `OutlinedTextField` (`imeField`, Material `label`, no placeholder), a nested modal, and tagged buttons and texts.
- **Number entry:** the `number-input` and `ofnumpad` screens. `NumberInputField` on iOS is a **native `UITextField` inside `UIKitView`, with `UIKitInteropProperties(isNativeAccessibilityEnabled = true)`**. The library sets `accessibilityIdentifier = "numberInput.field"` on every instance. The optional built-in keypad and accessory bar are Compose-drawn and tagged (`numberInput.keypad.<n>`, `numberInput.toolbar.*`).

Raw captures are in [`assets/compose-cmp/`](assets/compose-cmp/). `*-compact.json` is `snapshot-ui --output json`; `*-full.json` is the same with `--verbose`. Two `*-axe-describe-ui.json` files are raw `axe describe-ui --udid …` output from **the AXe binary bundled inside MobileBuildMCP** (`node_modules/mobilebuildmcp/bundled/axe`). They are read-only tree reads, taken to see nesting that rs/1 flattens. Every other device operation went through the MobileBuildMCP CLI.

## The specific checks

- **`testTag` as `identifier`: yes, unconditionally.** Every tag in the app arrived as `identifier`: on buttons, on plain `Text` (`outerTitle`, `perfModalTitle`), on the text field, and on untagged-role wrappers. In raw AXe it is `AXUniqueId`.
- **Roles.** Material `Button` is `button`; `Text`/`BasicText` is `text`; `OutlinedTextField` and native `UITextField` are `text-field`; `Image` with `contentDescription` is `image`; layouts, the `LazyColumn`, scroll containers and tagged wrappers are `other`; each Compose layer is a full-screen `scroll-view`; the app root is `application`.
- **Labels.** A button's label is its text content, and a text field's label is its Material `label` slot. A native `UITextField` has **no label**. An image's label is its `contentDescription`. Long texts are kept whole in full and compact captures.
- **Values.** A Compose text field reports its text as `value`. **An empty Compose field has no `value` key at all** (e25 in `03-…`). **An empty native `UITextField` reports its placeholder as `value`**: `"value":"Betrag eingeben"` in `07-…` e53, which is UIKit behaviour. Buttons and texts have no `value`.
- **Disabled state.** A disabled Compose button (keypad `backspace` and `decimal` in some states, toolbar `clear` and `toggleSign`) has `state.enabled: false` and `actions: []`, and drops out of compact targets.
- **Off-viewport and clipped Compose elements lose their label.** Tab buttons scrolled out of the horizontal row keep `identifier` but have **no `label`**, no `text` child, a 40 pt instead of 48 pt frame, `visible: false` and no actions (`01-…` e20). Scrolled back into view they have all of these again (`10-…` e18). The "Reset all" button, scrolled under the built-in keypad by the host's viewport padding, is worse: **`visible: true` with a `tap` action, but no label**, at a frame the keypad covers (`08-…` e55). A tap there would hit the keypad.
- **Duplicate identifiers.** `numberInput.field` appears five times on the number-input screen and three times on the OF numpad screen, because the library tags the component, not the instance. The app's unique per-field tags (`ofnumpad.amount` …) land on the `other` wrapper, which has no `typeText`.
- **Stale-element crash: not seen** (see Q8).
- **Hardware-keyboard state.** The simulator's software keyboard stays off-screen: its keys appear as `button`s at y ≥ 956 with `visible: false`, adding about 60 elements while a system-keyboard field is focused. Pressing Escape (HID 41) dismissed the whole Compose `Dialog` through its back handler, not just the edit menu.

## Implications for the developer guide

1. **CMP floor unchanged, and 1.11.1 was stable here.** Require ≥ 1.8.0 and recommend ≥ 1.12.1. Present the crash as a risk the owner's 1.11.1 app did not hit in about 150 operations, not as a demonstrated failure.
2. **Select Compose buttons by `identifier`, or by `role` + `label`, never by `label` alone in a guard.** Every button has a same-label `text` child. `resolveActionTarget` survives this (only the button has `tap`), but `assertScreenGuard` counts both visible matches and returns `GUARD_AMBIGUOUS` (`src/scripted/select.ts#L106-110`).
3. **Text fields.** `role: "text-field"` plus `identifier` works for Compose fields. Don't assert on "empty" through `value`: an empty Compose field has no `value`, but an empty native field reports its placeholder.
4. **Put the tag on the node that acts.** A tag on a wrapper (`UIKitView`, `Box`, `Column`) becomes an `other` that works as a screen guard but can't be tapped or typed into (`TARGET_UNAVAILABLE`). For UIKit views embedded in Compose, the identifier has to be set on the native view, per instance.
5. **Scroll before selecting, and don't trust labels of off-viewport elements.** Off-viewport Compose elements are present but unlabelled and not actionable. Elements clipped by a scroll viewport can still look tappable underneath an overlay.
6. **Swiping a Compose list.** The untagged `LazyColumn` has no `swipeWithin`; the swipe target on offer is the full-screen Compose-layer `scroll-view`. *Inference, not tested:* tagging the `LazyColumn` stops MobileBuildMCP pruning its inferred `swipeWithin`, because the prune only applies to `other` elements with no label, value or identifier.
7. **Dialogs.** A Compose `Dialog` hides the screen behind it from the tree, and each open dialog adds a full-screen `scroll-view`. Guards for the underlying screen should expect absence while a dialog is up.
8. **Replacing text** works through ⌘A in the default input mode, but it leaves the iOS edit menu open and shifts refs. Scripts should re-observe after `replaceText` (the bridge already captures before each step). Hardware Escape is not a safe way to close the menu.

## Implications for "Compose app evidence plan"

- **This app is a usable evidence app today:** offline, no signing team, a 31 s first build and a 6 s rebuild through MobileBuildMCP alone, rich tags, a full-screen modal, text and number entry, and a lazy list. It lacks Switch, `heading()`, native navigation or tab chrome, a placeholder field, and `usingNativeTextInput`. If those matter, they need a second app or a fixture (for example, Alkaa for lists and forms, and JetBrains `examples/chat` for a mixed shell).
- **Its default screen is a moving target.** `modal-perf` auto-runs about 25 s of dialog open and close on every launch and whenever the tab is re-entered. Scripts must either wait for "done (or idle)" first or navigate away from it immediately. It also makes a good built-in polling stress test for the crash question.
- **Number fields can't be addressed uniquely by identifier.** `numberInput.field` repeats, and the wrapper tags aren't actionable. A form-entry script against this app needs `identifier` + `value` (brittle) or a library change: a per-instance identifier or an accessibility label passed through `NumberInputField`. That change belongs to the library, not the bridge. The keypad keys (`numberInput.keypad.<n>`) are unique and tap cleanly, so keypad-driven entry works now.
- **No bridge change is indicated.** `identifier`, `role`, `label` and `value` plus full captures covered everything observed. The open items are guide text (points 2–8 above) and, optionally, better list-swipe inference, which is a MobileBuildMCP matter.
- **A planted-failure candidate:** a guard on `{label: "Close outer"}` without `role`. It must fail as `GUARD_AMBIGUOUS` because of the button-plus-text pair.
- **Still open:** the stale-element crash comparison needs a 1.12.1 build of the same app, which means a version bump, and this ticket forbade one. Switch and heading roles, and SwiftUI chrome in a mixed shell, also remain untested.
